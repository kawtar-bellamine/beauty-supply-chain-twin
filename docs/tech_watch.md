## Technology watch: where each innovation would act on this flow

The study shows where the days go (carrier transit 75 % of the lead time, a few sellers behind most handling delays,
a plant that cannot follow the Black Friday peak). Each technology below is mapped to one of those findings, with the
KPI a pilot would have to move. No vendor figure is quoted: the value has to be measured on our own data.

| Technology | Problem it addresses here | Pilot KPI |
|---|---|---|
| **Machine learning forecasting and demand sensing** | Weekly forecast error of 19 % at country level, 25 to 46 % by region | WAPE and bias by region, stock days of cover at constant service |
| **Digital twin of the network** (this project) | Choosing a regional DC, a pre-build plan or a safety level before spending | Fill rate, OTIF and cost per unit of each option, before and after |
| **IoT tracking of shipments** (GPS, temperature, shock) | Carrier transit is 75 % of the lead time and the main cause of late orders; fragrances and some formulas are sensitive to heat | Share of late orders detected before the promise date, claims for damaged goods |
| **RFID and inventory accuracy** | A fill rate is only as good as the stock record behind it | Inventory record accuracy, cycle-count time |
| **Warehouse robotics** (autonomous mobile robots, goods-to-person picking) | Handling time and the Black Friday capacity peak at the DC | Order lines picked per hour at peak, handling lead time p90 |
| **Control tower and alerting** | Nine sellers (1.5 %) cause half of the seller-side late orders | Late orders per partner, time to escalate |
| **Blockchain traceability** | Provenance of ingredients and protection of luxury products against counterfeits | Share of units traceable end to end, time to answer an audit |

Approach: one KPI per pilot, a baseline measured before, a control group where possible, and a go/no-go date.
